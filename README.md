# Hollowtide

*A Camel Studios game.* Website: **https://camells1.github.io/hollowtide/**

When the tide drains, the sunken ruins are yours for **two minutes**. Loot them and get out before the sea comes back.

## ⬇️ Download

- **Windows:** [**Hollowtide-Setup.exe**](https://github.com/Camells1/Hollowtide/releases/latest/download/Hollowtide-Setup.exe)
- **Chromebook / any browser:** [**Play in your browser**](https://camells1.github.io/play/hollowtide/). In Chrome you can click *Install* in the address bar to add it to your shelf.

> "Windows protected your PC"? The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## How it plays

One tide is about five minutes:

| Phase | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| High tide | 2:00 | You're on the island. Bank loot at the **Stash**, build upgrades at the **Workbench**. |
| Ebb | 0:20 | The lagoon drains and the ruins rise out of the water. Night falls. |
| **Low tide** | **2:00** | The Hollow is open: pry open the glowing chests, fight the hollow crabs. |
| Flood | 0:25 | A horn sounds and the sea rushes back. Swim for the island. The undertow pulls you down. |

If you drown, your pack sinks where you fell. Find it next low tide. Only loot in the Stash is safe.

**Upgrades:** Tide Lantern, Deep Lungs, Kelp Fins, Salvage Pack, Coral Blade, Tide Charm.

## Play together

- **Single player** or **co-op for up to 4** with a room code (Host Co-op → share the code → friends type it next to Join Co-op). No server to run.
- **Proximity voice chat:** hold **V** to talk (or switch to open mic in Settings). You hear friends from where they are; they fade out with distance and sound muffled under water.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD / mouse |
| Sprint (swim fast) | Shift |
| Jump (swim up) / dive | Space / C |
| Interact, hold to pry open chests | E |
| Swing your blade | Left click |
| Talk (push to talk) | V |
| Pause | Esc |
