How to play

1

High tide

You're on an island. Bank loot at the Stash and build upgrades at the Workbench.

2

The tide goes out

The sun sets, the lagoon drains and the ruins rise out of the water. Follow the beams of light.

3

Low tide

Run to the ruins and pry open the chests (hold E). Hollow crabs dig out of the sand to stop you.

4

The flood

Three bells, then the horn. Get to an island. Drown and your pack stays where you fell.
