HOLLOWTIDE

When the tide drains, the ruins are yours for two minutes.

A CAMEL STUDIOS GAME ·

How to play

  1. High tide (2 min): you're stuck on the island. Bank your loot at the Stash and buy upgrades at the Workbench.
  2. The tide goes out: the lagoon drains and the sunken ruins rise out of the water. It gets dark, and the Hollow glows.
  3. Low tide (2 min): run to the ruins and pry open the glowing chests (hold E). Watch out for hollow crabs.
  4. The flood: a horn sounds and the sea rushes back. Swim for the island. The undertow pulls you down, so mind your air.
  5. If you drown, your pack drops where you died. Find it next low tide. Only loot in the Stash is safe.
